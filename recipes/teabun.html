Teabun

A tea bun is a soft and lightly sweet baked bun that is perfect to enjoy with a cup of tea or coffee. It can be made with simple ingredients and has a soft texture with a slightly sweet flavor.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Add the butter and mix until the mixture becomes crumbly.
  4. Add the milk, egg, and vanilla extract.
  5. Mix the ingredients until a soft dough forms.
  6. Divide the dough into small portions and shape them into buns.
  7. Place the buns on a baking tray.
  8. Bake for about 15–20 minutes until the buns are lightly golden.
  9. Allow the buns to cool slightly before serving.
