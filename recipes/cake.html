This cake is soft, moist, and delicious. It is made with simple ingredients and is perfect for a sweet dessert.

Ingredients

  • Butter - 250g/li>
  • Sugar - 250g
  • Egg -8
  • Flour - 250g
  • Baking powder - 8g
  • Salt - 1.5g
  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease your baking pan and line it with parchment paper
  2. Sift together the flour, baking powder, and a pinch of salt in a separate bowl.
  3. Beat the softened butter and sugar together with an electric mixer until the mixture is pale, light, and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Then mix in the vanilla extract or essence.
  5. Alternate adding portions of the dry flour mixture and the milk to the wet ingredients, folding gently until just combined.
  6. Pour the batter into your prepared pan and bake for 35 to 50 minutes until a skewer inserted in the center comes out clean. Let it cool before serving.
