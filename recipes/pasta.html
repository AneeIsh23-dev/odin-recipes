Pasta

Pasta is a simple and delicious Italian dish made by cooking pasta noodles and combining them with a flavorful sauce, vegetables, herbs, and other ingredients. It is easy to prepare and can be customized with different sauces and toppings.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil and add a little salt.
  2. Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions until tender.
  3. Drain the pasta and set it aside.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the chopped garlic for a few seconds.
  5. Add the tomato sauce, salt, black pepper, and oregano.
  6. Simmer the sauce for a few minutes.
  7. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce and mix well.
  8. Serve the pasta with grated Parmesan cheese on top.
